# Firearm Safety Principles

BallisticsLab is educational. Real firearms should be handled only where lawful and under competent supervision appropriate to the user's age and local rules.

## Core rules

1. Treat every firearm as if it is loaded.
2. Keep the muzzle pointed in a safe direction.
3. Keep your finger outside the trigger guard until you are ready to fire under proper range conditions.
4. Know your target and what is beyond it.

## Range behavior

- Follow the range officer or instructor immediately.
- Do not handle firearms when the range is declared cold.
- Use appropriate eye and hearing protection where required.
- Do not rely on a mechanical safety as a substitute for safe handling.
- Stop if anything appears mechanically abnormal and hand the situation to the instructor/range staff.

## Learning boundary

This repository can cover physics, statistics, legal research, safety, history and high-level mechanism concepts.

It should not contain:

- weapon-construction instructions
- instructions to modify a firearm for greater lethality or rate of fire
- methods to defeat armour or safety systems
- illegal acquisition/carry guidance
- tactical instructions for harming people

The interesting part of engineering is understanding systems. Turning that into a criminal court exhibit is a remarkably low-return capstone project.
