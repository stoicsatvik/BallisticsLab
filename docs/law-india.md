# India Firearms Law — Reference Notes

This file is a study index, not legal advice. Firearms law changes, classifications matter, and secondary summaries can become stale. Prefer primary Government of India sources.

## Age and supervised training

The Ministry of Home Affairs FAQ summarizing Rule 36 states that a person below 21 but not below 12 may be allowed to use **permissible-category arms for training** in the immediate presence, or under the direct supervision and guidance, of an adult instructor or licensee.

Important distinction:

```text
supervised use for training != independent possession/carry/licensing
```

For any real-world training, confirm the current rule, firearm category, range/club authorization, instructor requirements and local procedure before acting.

## Classification matters

Do not reason from informal labels such as "pistol", "rifle" or "AK" alone. Indian law distinguishes categories of arms and ammunition, and the legal status can depend on calibre, action type, configuration and licensing context.

Study process:

1. identify the exact legal object/category
2. locate the controlling section/rule/schedule
3. check amendments and current notifications
4. distinguish possession, acquisition, transport, use and training
5. confirm with the competent authority or recognized shooting organization when acting in the real world

## Research questions

- What does the Arms Act define as an "arm", "firearm", "ammunition", "prohibited arm" and "prohibited ammunition"?
- How do permissible and restricted categories appear in the Arms Rules schedules?
- What does Rule 36 permit for training and sport shooting?
- What licensing forms apply to individuals, clubs and shooting associations?
- Which rules govern transport and storage?
